Privacy Policy

AQUASCOUT

Last updated 10 October 2026 · version 1.51 · package com.scc.aquascout

The short version: your camera video is analysed on your phone and thrown away. It is never uploaded. The only things that can leave your device are a detection label, a confidence number and your coordinates, and only if you have turned on alerts and signed in.

What we do not do

  • We do not record or store video.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data. There is no advertising SDK in this app.
  • We do not collect your contacts, your photos gallery or your microphone.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Why the app asks for permissions

Each permission is used for one thing and nothing else. Android requires us to explain this, and we would rather over-explain than leave you guessing.

  • Camera — to look for fire and smoke. Without it there is nothing to detect.
  • Location — to attach your coordinates to the alert SMS. Optional; alerts still send without it.
  • Notifications — to tell you a fire was found. Optional.
  • Bluetooth — to control the optional rover. The app works fully with no rover.
  • Internet — only for Google sign-in and syncing your alert history. Detection itself needs no network.

Third parties

Two, and both are optional:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in and alert history, if you use it.
  • Semaphore — SMS delivery, if you enter an API key. Your key goes from your phone to Semaphore and back. It is not sent to us.

SMS alerts

When an alert fires the app sends one text message to the emergency contact you saved. The message contains what was detected, a confidence score and your coordinates. A 30-second cooldown stops it sending repeatedly. Standard carrier rates may apply to the message you receive.

Deleting your data

Alerts stored in your Firebase account are removed by deleting the account from within the app, or by signing out and asking us. Everything held on the device disappears when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Children

AQUASCOUT is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information about them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and ship the new version with the change.
