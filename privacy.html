Policy

What we store, and what we don't

AQUASCOUT was built so the camera stays on the phone. This page lists every place data can leave the device, in plain terms.

The one thing that never leaves

Camera frames are analysed by a 3.3 MB model running on the phone's own processor. Frames are not uploaded, not cached to a server, and not used for training. If the phone is offline, detection still works.

No image ever leaves the device

Not to us, not to Firebase, not to anyone. Ever.

What does leave the device

  • Your alert log. If you sign in, detection times, labels and confidence scores sync to Firebase so you can read them on a second device. If you stay signed out, the log stays on the phone.
  • An SMS. If you turn SMS on and enter an API key, a detection sends a message to the one number you typed, containing the label, the confidence and the phone's GPS position. That message goes through Semaphore.
  • Your account. If you sign in, Firebase holds your email address and your user ID. We can see account emails, but not your alert data unless you are signed in and syncing it.

Location

The GPS position is added only to an outgoing alert SMS, and only at the moment of sending. It is not stored on our servers and it is not written into the synced alert log. Deny the location permission and alerts still fire, just without coordinates.

Two known weaknesses in this build

We would rather list these than have you find them. Both are fixed in the next release.

  • The Semaphore API key is stored in plain app preferences. It is readable on a rooted or backed-up device. Move it to Android's encrypted storage before any public release.
  • Android backup is currently enabled. App data can be extracted over USB with a debug tool. Disabling it is scheduled for the next build.

Getting rid of your data

Signing out clears the local session. To remove synced alert records for good, delete the account in Profile; that drops your Firebase user path and everything under it.
