About

AQUASCOUT

Built by zham · Saint Columban College · package com.scc.aquascout

The short version: a fire and smoke detector that runs entirely on an Android phone. A 3.3 MB YOLOv8n model watches the camera at about 2 frames per second, entirely on-device. Nothing is uploaded. When it fires, it texts one number you choose with your location attached.

Who made this

I built it as a Computer Science undergraduate project at Saint Columban College. The Android app is one half; this site and the 3D rover prototype are the other.

The reasoning was simple. Smoke detectors are expensive, and the useful ones need mains power or a battery that dies in the worst month. A phone is already in your hand, already charged, and already has a camera pointed at the ceiling. The hard part was never the idea, it was making detection fast enough and small enough to run on a handset without draining it.

How it works

StepWhat happens
DetectTwo classes only, fire and smoke. A focused model does one job better than a general one.
AlertSMS to one number you choose, with the phone's GPS pin. Any carrier, and nothing to install on the receiving end.
StoreFirebase keeps your alert log. It never sees your camera.
OverrideSmell smoke before the camera sees it? Hold the red button for a second and a half.

The architecture diagram walks through the trust boundary, which is the part worth understanding: frames are read into a buffer, classified, and discarded. The only things that can leave the device are a label, a confidence number and your coordinates. The privacy policy covers the rest.

What it still gets wrong

Candles, cigarettes and steam scored high enough to trigger on an earlier build and still need re-testing. This is the failure mode that matters most in a house, and it is not solved. The sensitivity threshold is adjustable in Settings, which moves the tradeoff around rather than removing it.

Everything here is measured on a held-out split, not on a real house. Real rooms have moving shadows, changing light and steam that a static test set does not. Treat these numbers as a floor, not a promise.

  • Two classes. No LPG, no chemical, no electrical arcing.
  • About 2 frames per second. A fast flame can start between frames.
  • The model is small on purpose. Accuracy is the price of that.
  • Static analysis only, no live debugging.
