What it does

01

On-device detection

A custom-trained YOLOv8n INT8 model runs entirely on your phone's CPU. No image is uploaded to any server, ever.

02

Smoke and fire only

Two-class model trained on a focused fire and smoke dataset. No noisy person-or-vehicle classes. The alert means what it says.

03

SMS alert with GPS

On detection, the app can send an SMS to your emergency contact with the phone's GPS location.

04

Two-tier privacy

Camera, AI, and alert log work without sign-in. Only cloud sync of alert history needs a Firebase account.

05

Phone alone is enough

The app is useful with no rover, no extra camera, no extra battery. Open it, point it, get a verdict, close it.

06

Manual alert button

See or smell smoke? Tap the red button on the Live screen to send an alert without waiting for the AI. Both paths go to the same alert system.

07

Free, no subscription

Single APK download. The only paid cost is your own Semaphore credits for SMS, if you turn that feature on.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open the app and grant camera access.

    The first run is one tap. No account needed to start detecting.

  2. 2

    Point the camera at the area you want to watch.

    The live view runs the YOLOv8n model on each frame. A box and confidence label appear when fire or smoke is detected.

  3. 3

    Get the alert, on screen or by SMS.

    The on-screen alert always fires. With an emergency contact and Semaphore API key in Settings, an SMS with the GPS pin is also sent.

  4. 4

    Check the alert log and chart when you want.

    Each detection is timestamped and saved locally. Sign in to sync the log to Firebase and see it on a second device.
