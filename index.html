What it does

Detects
Two classes only, fire and smoke. A focused model does one job better than a general one.
Alerts
SMS to one number you choose, with the phone's GPS pin. Works on any carrier, needs no app on the receiving end.
Keeps quiet
Frames never leave the handset. Firebase stores your alert log, not your camera.
Lets you override
Smell smoke before the camera sees it? Hold the red button for a second and a half.

Interactive

Orbit the rover and drive it

A live 3D model of the LAFVIN 2WD chassis, with the Arduino, the JDY-16 link, two ESP32-CAM heads and the water pump. Drag to orbit, use the arrows to drive, and turn the pump on.
