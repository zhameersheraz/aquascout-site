What it does

01

On-device detection

Custom YOLOv8n INT8 model on the phone CPU. No image ever leaves the device.

02

Smoke and fire only

Two-class model trained on a focused dataset. The alert means what it says.

03

SMS alert with GPS

On detection, send an SMS to your emergency contact with the phone's GPS pin.

04

Two-tier privacy

Camera, AI, and alerts work without sign-in. Cloud sync is the only thing that needs an account.

05

Phone alone is enough

No rover, no extra camera, no extra battery. Open it, point it, get a verdict, close it.

06

Manual alert button

See or smell smoke? Tap the red button. Both AI and manual paths go to the same alert system.

07

Free, no subscription

One APK. No account wall. The only cost is your own Semaphore credits, if you turn SMS on.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open the app and grant camera access.

    The first run is one tap. No account needed to start detecting.

  2. 2

    Point the camera at the area you want to watch.

    The live view runs the YOLOv8n model on each frame. A box and confidence label appear when fire or smoke is detected.

  3. 3

    Get the alert, on screen or by SMS.

    The on-screen alert always fires. With an emergency contact and Semaphore API key in Settings, an SMS with the GPS pin is also sent.

  4. 4

    Check the alert log and chart when you want.

    Each detection is timestamped and saved locally. Sign in to sync the log to Firebase and see it on a second device.
