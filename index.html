What it does

Four jobs, done on the phone.

Detects
Two classes only, fire and smoke. A focused model does one job better than a general one.
Alerts
SMS to one number you choose, with the phone's GPS pin. Works on any carrier, needs no app on the receiving end.
Keeps quiet
Frames never leave the handset. Firebase stores your alert log, not your camera. See the trust boundary
Lets you override
Smell smoke before the camera sees it? Hold the red button for a second and a half.

How it works

Three steps, no server in the middle.

  1. STEP 01

    The phone looks

    The camera feed goes straight into the TFLite interpreter. No upload, no round trip, no waiting on a signal.

  2. STEP 02

    The model decides

    Boxes above your confidence threshold become detections, each one labelled fire or smoke with a score you can see.

  3. STEP 03

    Somebody is told

    If you do not acknowledge it, the alert escalates by SMS to your emergency contact with your coordinates attached.
