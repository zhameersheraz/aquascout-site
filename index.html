What it does

Four jobs, done on the phone.

  1. 01

    Detects

    Two classes only, fire and smoke. A focused model does one job better than a general one.

    ON DEVICE
  2. 02

    Alerts

    SMS to one number you choose, with the phone's GPS pin. Works on any carrier, needs no app on the receiving end.

    SMS + GPS
  3. 03

    Keeps quiet

    Frames never leave the handset. Firebase stores your alert log, not your camera. See the trust boundary

    NO UPLOAD
  4. 04

    Lets you override

    Smell smoke before the camera sees it? Hold the red button for a second and a half.

    HOLD 1.5s

How it works

Three steps, no server in the middle.

  1. 01

    The phone looks

    The camera feed goes straight into the TFLite interpreter. No upload, no round trip, no waiting on a signal.

  2. 02

    The model decides

    Boxes above your confidence threshold become detections, each one labelled fire or smoke with a score you can see.

  3. 03

    Somebody is told

    If you do not acknowledge it, the alert escalates by SMS to your emergency contact with your coordinates attached.

Install on Google Play

Recommended. AQUASCOUT is live on Google Play in 177 countries. Open the listing, tap Install, and the Play Store handles the rest. This is the same 1.56 build that ran in closed testing since 23 October.

Download APK 1.56

The same 1.56 build, sideloaded (39 MB). No Google account needed. Your browser will ask once, the first time, to allow installs from it. Installing this way does not give you Play Store updates.

Already installed it from Google Play? Uninstall that one first. Play signs its build with its own key, so Android refuses to install one over the other.

To send an SMS alert, paste your Semaphore API key into Settings first. Without it the detector still runs and logs detections, but no message is ever sent.
